Support

Need help with BetPath? This page provides general information about the application and its functionality.

What is BetPath?

BetPath is a mathematical bankroll and staking calculator based on the Masaniello method. It helps users set up, calculate and track a progression manually.

What BetPath Does

  • Calculates Masaniello progressions.
  • Tracks the bankroll during a progression.
  • Recalculates stakes after recorded results.
  • Allows individual event odds to be adjusted.
  • Stores progressions locally on the device.
  • Provides progression summaries and CSV export.

What BetPath Does Not Do

  • BetPath does not accept or place bets.
  • BetPath does not process gambling payments.
  • BetPath does not connect to bookmakers.
  • BetPath does not provide sports predictions.
  • BetPath does not guarantee winnings or profits.
Responsible use: The Masaniello method does not guarantee winnings or profits. Betting involves the risk of financial loss. Use BetPath responsibly and only where permitted by applicable law.
